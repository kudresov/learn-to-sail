How the drills work

One skill at a time

Each tutorial is a ladder of stages. A stage adds one idea, and you clear it by getting 8 of your last 10 right, not by reading about it.

Consequences, not scores

Where it makes sense the drill shows what the mistake does on the water: how far off track, which bank you hit, which vessel you cut across.

Say the rule first

Before the number you call the rule. Recall of the reason is what survives to exam day and to the cockpit.

Yours, in your browser

Progress, streaks and the ship's log live in this browser. No accounts. Clear site data and you start as a Deckhand again.
