The ladder

TvariationMdeviationC

True Virgins Make Dull Company. True → Variation → Magnetic → Deviation → Compass. Every question is one or two steps on this ladder, in one direction or the other.

Going down (chart → compass)

West is best, East is least. Add a westerly error, subtract an easterly one. …add Whisky: True Virgins Make Dull Company Add Whisky (West).

045°T + 4°W = 049°M + 2°W = 051°C

Going up (compass → chart)

CADET: Compass, ADd East, True. Add an easterly error, subtract a westerly one, the exact mirror of going down.

051°C − 2°W = 049°M − 4°W = 045°T

Which compass?

Steering compass: variation and deviation. It lives next to the engine and the speakers.

Hand-bearing compass: variation only. You take it away from the boat's iron, so no deviation card. Examiners love this trap.

Why one degree matters

Off by 1° over 6 nm and you are 0.1 nm (a cable) off track. Off by 6° and you are over half a mile out. On the passage you will see exactly where that puts you.

Habits examiners like

Always three figures: 007°, not 7. Always a suffix: T, M or C. Wrap through 360: 358 + 5 = 003. Look up deviation by the ship's heading, never by the bearing of an object.
