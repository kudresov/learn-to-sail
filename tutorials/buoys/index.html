Is there any red port left? The rule follows the direction of buoyage: from seaward into harbour, up rivers and estuaries, clockwise round a landmass, or the magenta arrow on the chart. Going the other way, flip it. IALA B (the Americas, Japan, Korea, the Philippines) is the mirror image.

The cones point to where the black is. The flashes are a clock face: 3 at three o'clock, 6 at six, 9 at nine, continuous at twelve. South's long flash stops six being misread as nine.

No colour letter means white. The period (10s) runs from the start of one sequence to the start of the next.

Fl(3) WRG 15s 21m 18M

Groups of three flashes, white, red and green sectors depending on your bearing, every 15 seconds, the light 21 metres above high water, nominal range 18 nautical miles.
