Forecast forces are the mean wind. Gusts are stronger.

    
Forecast words

    
Imminent within 6 h of issue · soon 6–12 h · later more than 12 h. Gale F8 or gusts 43–51 kn · severe gale F9 or gusts 52–60 · storm F10 or gusts 61–68 · violent storm F11 or gusts 69+ · hurricane force F12.

    
Visibility: good more than 5 nm · moderate 2–5 nm · poor 1000 m – 2 nm · very poor less than 1000 m. Sea: smooth <0.5 m · slight 0.5–1.25 · moderate 1.25–2.5 · rough 2.5–4 · very rough 4–6 · high 6–9 m.

    
Veer and back

    
Wind is named for where it blows from. Veer = clockwise (SW → W). Back = anticlockwise (SW → S). Directions in three figures, true: 270°T.

    
The glass, change in 3 h

    
Slowly 0.1–1.5 mb · plain rising/falling 1.6–3.5 · quickly 3.6–6.0 · very rapidly more than 6.0. Systems move slowly <15 kn · steadily 15–25 · rather quickly 25–35 · rapidly 35–45 · very rapidly 45+. Long foretold, long last; short notice, soon past.

    
Isobars and Buys Ballot

    
Northern hemisphere: wind goes anticlockwise round a low, clockwise round a high, angled about 15° in towards the low over the sea. Close isobars, strong wind. Back to the wind, low on your left.

    
A depression passing north of you

    
High cloud thickens, glass falls, wind backs and freshens. Warm front: steady rain, wind veers, glass steadies. Warm sector: mild, drizzle, poor visibility. Cold front: squalls, heavy rain, wind veers sharply, glass rises. Behind: showers, gusty northwesterly, good visibility.

    
Fog and breezes

    
Advection fog: warm moist air over cold sea, spring and early summer, a breeze does not clear it. Radiation fog: clear calm night over land, burns off in the morning. Sea breeze: sunny day, onshore by afternoon, dies at sunset. Wind against tide: short steep seas.
